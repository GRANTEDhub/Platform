Preventing Drug Overdoses: Community Prevention and Response

SAMHSA is funding community-wide programmes that reduce overdose deaths by expanding access to FDA-approved reversal medications. Awardees purchase and distribute naloxone through existing outreach channels, train both laypeople and prescribers on overdose recognition and response, and stand up at least one population-level prevention strategy — most commonly fake-pill awareness.
